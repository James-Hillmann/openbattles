Controls

Select
Click a unit or building. Drag a box around your units. Shift adds to the selection.
Orders
Right-click the ground to move, an enemy to attack, a tree to chop it (Builders), your unfinished building to help build it.
Build / train
Select a Builder, Castle or Barracks, then click an icon on the red strip or on Build Costs above. Place a building with a click; right-click or Esc cancels.
Camera
Arrow keys or WASD, middle-drag or Space + drag, mouse wheel to zoom.
